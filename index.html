The festival

Five days of light

Deepavali is a festival of five days, and the lamps stay lit for all of them.

  1. Dhanteras

    The start of the festival. New beginnings, a new utensil or coin, and a lamp at the door.

  2. Naraka Chaturdashi

    Also called Choti Diwali. Lamps at dusk, rangoli at the threshold and the first sweets.

  3. The night itself

    Lakshmi Puja

    Diwali night. Homes glow, prayers are offered to Lakshmi and Ganesha, and sweets are shared.

  4. Govardhan Puja

    Annakut, a mountain of food offered in gratitude, and the start of a new year in many homes.

  5. Bhai Dooj

    Brothers and sisters celebrate their bond with tilak, gifts and a meal together.

Happy Diwali
